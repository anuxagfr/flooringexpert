Laminate Flooring
Classic Aesthetics. AC4 Durability.

Elevate your space with Bekker's premium 8mm laminate collections. Featuring a high-density fiberboard (HDF) core, commercial-grade AC4 wear rating, and beautifully detailed European wood textures.

Explore Collections View Advantages

Built for Life's High Traffic

Bekker Laminate flooring relies on an exceptionally dense HDF core to prevent denting and provide dimensional stability. The transparent AC4 top layer guards against scratching, fading, and everyday wear in both bustling homes and commercial settings.

Key Features:

  • AC4 Rating: Highly resistant to micro-scratches from pets, heels, and furniture.
  • Click-Lock System: Fast, glue-less floating floor installation.
  • UV Protection: Colors won't fade or yellow under direct sunlight.

Standard vs. WV4 Grooved Edges

  • Standard Series (Seamless)

    Planks fit flush together creating a smooth, continuous surface ideal for a sleek, modern aesthetic and exceptionally easy cleaning.

  • WV4 Series (V-Groove)

    Features a subtle bevel on all four sides. When clicked together, it forms a V-shaped groove that meticulously replicates the look of traditional solid hardwood planks.
