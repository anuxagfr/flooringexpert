Industrial Strength. Modern Aesthetics.

Ultra-lightweight, highly durable, and visually striking. Elevate your architectural designs with our premium Aluminum Foam panels, perfect for facades, ceilings, and feature walls.

Redefining Modern Architecture

Aluminum foam represents the intersection of industrial strength and contemporary aesthetics. Its unique cellular structure absorbs sound, resists fire, and provides excellent thermal insulation while maintaining an incredibly light profile.

Key Highlights:

  • Lightweight: Drastically reduces structural load.
  • Non-Combustible: High fire resistance for ultimate safety.

Performance Features:

  • Superior acoustic absorption
  • Excellent thermal insulation
  • High impact and crash resistance
  • 100% recyclable and eco-friendly
  • Available in various translucency levels

Why Choose Aluminum Foam?

Acoustic Control

The open-hole structure offers excellent sound absorption for high-traffic interiors.

Fire Resistance

Non-combustible material with high fire resistance suitable for industrial and commercial use.

Ultra Lightweight

High strength-to-weight ratio (Density 0.2-8 g/cm³), making installation effortless.

Light Transmission

The porous structure allows for unique backlit effects and light diffusion.

Product Specifications FAQ

Can the foam be painted or colored?

Is it suitable for outdoor environments?

Absolutely. Its high strength and durable eco-friendly properties make it perfect for exterior facades.
